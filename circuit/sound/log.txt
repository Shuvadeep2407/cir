## 1. Project Info
- **Project:** C-UAS Training Aid Demo — Acoustic Detection Subsystem
- **Circuit:** Custom STM32F412RCT6-based acoustic sensor node with 8x INMP441 digital MEMS microphones + 200 mm parabolic reflector
- **Quantity:** 3 identical PCBs (one per cluster: F1 @ 60°, F2 @ 180°, F3 @ 300°)
- **Location:** Fixed acoustic layer at 1.20 m height, 200 mm radius from center hub
- **Last Updated:** 2026-06-28
- **Status:** Power supply section placed on schematic — EUP3484A buck converter (24V→3.3V). STM32F412RCT6 and INMP441 microphones not yet added.

## 3. Link / Connection Status
- 8x INMP441 - I2S bus (WS, SCK, SD) - STM32F412RCT6
- STM32F412RCT6 - UART or SPI - LoRa module - AI workstation / console
- Power: 3.3 VDC (from main-circuit power supply via 24 VDC bus + local regulator)
- Processing: STM32F412RCT6 runs FFT / beamforming (DoA) - packages data - LoRa uplink
